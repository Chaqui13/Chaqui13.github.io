Privacy Policy — The Ezra Files Automation

Last updated: July 27, 2026

The Ezra Files Automation is a personal, single-user automation tool built and operated by the creator of The Ezra Files (@theezrafiles) to publish that creator's own video content to their own TikTok account.

What this application does

This application uploads pre-produced video content, created by the account owner, directly to the TikTok account owned and operated by the same individual, using TikTok's Content Posting API. It is not a public-facing service, does not have other users or accounts, and is not distributed to or used by any third party.

Data we access

Through TikTok's API, this application accesses only the data necessary to authenticate as the account owner and to publish video content on their behalf (OAuth access/refresh tokens for the connected TikTok account).

Data we do not collect

This application does not collect, store, process, or share any personal data belonging to TikTok users, viewers, or any third party. It does not access follower lists, direct messages, analytics of other accounts, or any data beyond what is required to publish the account owner's own content.

Data storage

OAuth credentials are stored locally and privately by the account owner, on infrastructure they personally control. They are never transmitted to or shared with any third party beyond TikTok's own API.

Third-party sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. No data collected through this integration is used for advertising, analytics resale, or any purpose other than publishing the account owner's own content to their own TikTok account.

Contact

For any questions about this policy, contact the account owner via the contact details associated with the TikTok account @theezrafiles.
